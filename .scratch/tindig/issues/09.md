# 09 — Record owner-confirmed GCash purchases

Status: ready-for-agent | Published: https://github.com/Shirooo098/Aira/issues/9

Suggested lead: D3. Developer numbers are roles, not assigned GitHub accounts. Each lead owns a complete UI/action/persistence/verification slice and coordinates shared schema changes with D3.

## What to build

Prepare a GCash sale, keep it pending while the owner checks receipt of funds, then confirm it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Clearly distinguish pending payment and owner-confirmed payment; a screenshot cannot independently confirm receipt.
- [ ] Save the sale/payment/stock once on owner confirmation; pending drafts do not masquerade as completed sales.
- [ ] Preserve cash behavior, show payment method in sale history, and surface interrupted/failed confirmation.
- [ ] Test repeated confirmation, restart behavior, cash/GCash separation and absence of automatic payment/refund execution.

No silent fallback, secret exposure, autonomous payment verification, or unconfirmed financial writes. Preserve unrelated changes. No commit/push unless separately requested.
