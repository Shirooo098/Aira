# Aira

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

One owner-operated sari-sari store, initially using one Oppo Reno6 Z 5G. The owner serves customers while looking up notebook prices, recording purchases and payments, maintaining stock, and tracking customer credit.

## Product Purpose

Aira (Artificial Intelligence for Retail Assistance) reduces the effort of store recordkeeping through Filipino/Taglish speech, familiar product aliases, and typing. Success means reliable price lookup and correct, reviewable records without an internet connection.

## Positioning

The intended distinction is on-device speech, owner-confirmed store vocabulary, and low-effort capture with visible correction. This is a product direction, not a verified claim of uniqueness or measured superiority.

## Operating Context

- Core workflows must work in airplane mode with Wi-Fi disabled. Required production models ship with the installation; no silent cloud fallback or external model download.
- Ask price, Sell, and Manage products have visible mode indicators and short audio cues. Changing modes does not save records.
- Voice and typing support capture and correction. Price answers come from the catalog; unknown or ambiguous products require explicit resolution.
- The owner checks their own GCash account before confirming payment. Receipt images and OCR are supporting evidence, never payment authentication.
- Philippine calendar periods use Asia/Manila for Today, Week, Month, 6 Months, and Year reports.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Agreed scope: catalog and aliases, price lookup, reviewed sales, inventory, cash and GCash records, utang, reporting, and guarded reporting/restock assistance. Scope does not imply these features are implemented.
- Speech reliability and confirmed aliases take priority. Notebook OCR is a bounded experiment and must not block the voice workflow.
- AI proposes drafts. The owner reviews and confirms changes; a sale requires an explicit final confirmation button.
- Money is stored and calculated as integer centavos. Financial arithmetic and prices are not generated by models.
- Confirmed sale, payment/debt, and inventory changes must be atomic and protected against duplicate confirmation. Cancellation restores stock exactly once and preserves history.
- Add-delivery and set-count are separate actions. Catalog price changes and approved restock checklists do not implicitly change stock.
- Utang supports partial payments, reviewed opening balances, oldest-first repayments, and balance-preserving reversals. Repayments are collections, not additional sales; opening balances do not create sales or stock movements.
- Debt attention bands are Current (under 3 days), Needs attention (3–6 days), Urgent (7+ days), and Age unknown. Partial repayment does not reset remaining debt age.
- Reports distinguish sales, collections, outstanding credit, and stock now. Missing history must be visible; no profit claims without cost and expense data.
- No multi-device sync, autonomous payment verification, transfers/refunds, automatic debtor messages, or automatic supplier orders.
- Open decisions remain governed by TINDIG-SPEC.md, including unknown-date debt ordering, some reversal/report attribution semantics, backup policy, final model choices, and performance acceptance methodology.

## Product Principles

1. Keep selling possible without connectivity.
2. Let the owner understand, correct, and confirm every consequential change.
3. Preserve accurate money, stock, and debt records with visible history.
4. Surface uncertainty and failed operations instead of guessing or silently recovering.
5. Prioritize reliable everyday capture over experimental automation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide readable controls, tappable choices, and recoverable capture failures. Keep typing and correction available alongside speech. Pair audio cues with visible mode indicators; never communicate debt priority through color alone. Preserve Android accessibility and responsive behavior.
